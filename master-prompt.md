**Project Type:** Minecraft Mod

**Core Themes:** Olympian gods, legendary heroes, mythical creatures, ancient civilizations, divine magic, heroic quests, monumental architecture, and the Underworld.

---

# PART I — MASTER VISION

## 1. Project Overview

Design and develop an enormous Greek mythology expansion for Minecraft that transforms the game into a living world inspired by ancient Greek mythology.

OLYMPIA should feel like stepping into an interactive mythological world.

Imagine:
- Massive white marble temples overlooking turquoise oceans.
- Mount Olympus towering above the clouds.
- Enormous statues of Zeus, Athena, and Poseidon.
- Ancient Greek cities filled with merchants, philosophers, warriors, and priests.
- Beautiful Mediterranean islands containing forgotten civilizations.
- Enormous underground labyrinths.
- The Minotaur stalking players through dark corridors.
- Centaurs roaming sacred forests.
- Cyclopes forging legendary weapons inside volcanic mountains.
- Harpies nesting on distant cliffs.
- Pegasus flying above ancient temples.
- The Oracle of Delphi revealing mysterious prophecies.
- Atlantis hidden beneath the ocean.
- Hades ruling over an enormous supernatural Underworld.
- Ancient Titans imprisoned beneath the world.

The player begins as an ordinary mortal and gradually becomes a legendary Greek hero.

The ultimate goal is to create a substantial, polished, independently playable mythology mod.

## 2. Core Design Philosophy

The mod must prioritize five pillars:

**Exploration**
The world should constantly reward curiosity. Every mountain, island, temple, cave, and ruin should have the potential to contain something interesting.

**Mythology**
Use recognizable Greek mythology, characters, stories, artifacts, creatures, and architecture. Avoid making this a generic fantasy mod with Greek names attached.

**Adventure**
Major locations should contain memorable encounters, environmental puzzles, unique bosses, hidden rooms, and treasure.

**Progression**
Players should gradually earn divine favor, discover powerful equipment, unlock new crafting systems, and access increasingly dangerous locations.

**Living Civilization**
Greek settlements should feel populated. NPCs should have jobs, schedules, personalities, interactions, activities, and relationships with the surrounding world.

The mod must complement vanilla Minecraft rather than replace its core survival mechanics.
