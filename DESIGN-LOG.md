

## Run: Extend (a mount: the Pegasus)

**Brief:** a Pegasus based on the horse, with the other mounts. **Built:** `Pegasus` (hand-written on `AbstractHorse`: tame it by riding it again and again, saddle it, feed it; once saddled and ridden it flies: hold jump to climb, look down while moving to dive, let go to hover, no fall damage), registered in `OlympiaEntities` with an egg and a rare spawn in meadows and cherry groves, and a 43-part model (`Rig.horse` plus `Rig.wings`, a purple saddle blanket, a gold mane) generated from `mobs/pegasus.json`. **Checked:** it builds, loads, summons, can be ridden by command (`ride`), and was photographed from six angles. **Not checked:** taming, flight and steering were not driven (no keyboard); the wings' beat in the air was not seen, only their raised resting pose; the first wing designs folded back into a T-pose and were replaced by a raised pose baked into the model.
