Places

Ten things in the grass

Seven temples, a roadside shrine, an olive grove and a ruin. Each is a template with variants, generated by the game where the biome allows, with a chest of themed loot. The labyrinth has its own page.

Temple of Zeus

A peristyle temple of Doric columns on three steps, a pitched roof of terracotta tiles, gold in the pediment, and inside the altar of Zeus between two braziers, with a chest. One variant is whole, one ruined.

Temple of Zeus: facts
Size15 by 23 blocks, 17 high; 2 variants
SpacingOne in every 34 by 34 chunks
Biomesplains, meadow, sunflower plains
GuardiansHoplite (1 to 2). Set in the structure’s data; not yet watched in play.
ChestGold Ingot, Emerald, Lightning Rod, Thunder Shard, White Marble, Ambrosia, Obol, Potion of Zeus’s Fury, Thunderbolt

Status

Seen in a running Minecraft 1.21.4 game:

  • Zeus, Athena, the shrine, the grove and the ruin generate on their own in a flat plains world: the game’s /locate found each, from 97 to 273 blocks away. The temples of Apollo, Ares and Demeter were also found by /locate (249 to 416 blocks away); Artemis’s needs a forest and Poseidon’s a beach, so those two were placed by command.
  • All of them were also placed with the template command and photographed from a client.

Not yet tested:

  • The Temple of Poseidon generating on a beach on its own.
  • How these sit among vanilla villages and terrain (a flat test world has none).
  • Cities, Delphi, harbours and Mount Olympus are not in this slice.
