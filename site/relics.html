Relics & potions

A thunderbolt, a thread, and ambrosia

Relics

The numbers here are as written in the data; the relics were exercised through a command that runs the same code as a right-click.

  • Thunderbolt of Zeus

    Hurls lightning where you look (up to 48 blocks), with 10 extra damage to anything near the strike. It works only once Zeus has named you Favored; before that, it refuses. It wears out after 64 uses and needs 6 seconds between throws.

    Found in 35% of the Temple of Zeus’s chests.

  • Thread of Ariadne

    Use it to tie the thread where you stand. Use it again anywhere (in the same dimension) and it says which way the knot lies, how far, and how high, with a line of light along the first stretch. Sneak and use to tie it somewhere new.

    In a chest in the labyrinth pavilion.

  • Ambrosia

    Food of the gods: eat it at any hunger for 6 food points and Regeneration II for 20 seconds. Shapeless: Sacred Olive, Honey Bottle, Gold Nugget.

Drops

  • Obol

    A bronze coin dropped by hoplites and found in chests. It has no use yet: it is for the Underworld’s ferryman, which is not in this slice.

  • Cyclops Bloodstone

    A red stone dropped by cyclopes and found in labyrinth vaults. No use yet.

  • Minotaur Horn

    Always dropped by the Minotaur. No use yet.

The five potions

  • Ambrosia

    Olympus, Food of the Gods. Regeneration II for 2:00. Absorption I for 2:00.

    Brew an Awkward Potion with Sacred Olive. Found in an olive grove.

    A sacred olive, pressed once. The gods’ own mending.

  • Zeus’s Fury

    Zeus, King of the Gods. Strength II for 3:00. Resistance I for 3:00.

    Brew an Awkward Potion with Thunder Shard. Found in the Temple of Zeus.

    A shard of cooled lightning. Strength, and a heart that does not flinch.

  • Poseidon’s Breath

    Poseidon, Earth-Shaker. Water Breathing I for 5:00. Dolphin’s Grace I for 5:00.

    Brew an Awkward Potion with Sea Pearl. Found in the Temple of Poseidon.

    A pearl from the deep. Breathe water, swim like a dolphin.

  • Athena’s Wisdom

    Athena, Grey-Eyed. Luck II for 5:00. Night Vision I for 5:00.

    Brew an Awkward Potion with Owl Feather. Found in the Temple of Athena.

    A feather of the grey owl. Luck for the prepared mind.

  • Hermes’s Swiftness

    Hermes, Messenger of the Gods. Speed III for 3:00. Slow Falling I for 3:00.

    Brew an Awkward Potion with Winged Feather. Found in a shrine of Hermes.

    A feather from a winged sandal. Speed, and a soft landing.

The potions use the same data format as Asgard’s and Khemet’s: one entry in potions.json adds an ingredient, a potion and a brewing recipe.

Status

Seen in a running Minecraft 1.21.4 game:

  • The Thunderbolt refused at Unknown Mortal, struck at Favored (a lightning bolt appeared; the item took one point of damage).
  • The Thread of Ariadne tied at one point and then said \u201cnorth-west, 50 blocks\u201d and \u201csouth-east, 25 blocks\u201d from two other points, both correct.
  • The five potions and their ingredients load, and the recipes register.

Not yet tested:

  • A right-click with the Thunderbolt or the thread by hand.
  • Eating ambrosia.
  • Brewing, drinking or splashing the potions.
  • Any use for the obol, the bloodstone and the horn.
