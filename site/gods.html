Gods

Eight of the Twelve

The gods here are game material: a simplified retelling for a Minecraft mod, made with respect for the ancient Greek world it draws on, and not a statement about religion.

Each god has a temple or shrine and an altar. Hold an offering, use the altar, and the god takes one item, adds to your favor and gives you the blessings your stage has earned (tested through the matching command; the click itself is not yet tried). Favor is saved with the player.

Favor

  1. 0Unknown Mortal
  2. 3Recognized
  3. 8Favored
  4. 18Blessed
  5. 36Champion of Olympus

Use an altar with an empty hand to see your favor and what the god wants. /olympia favor lists your standing with every god. Zeus’s Thunderbolt works only once Zeus has named you Favored.

Status

Seen in a running Minecraft 1.21.4 game:

  • Offering, refusal of the wrong item and favor kept per god, through the command that runs the same code as the altar: Zeus 1 and 2 favor for gold ingots, Poseidon refused gold, Athena accepted a book.
  • At 37 favor (Champion of Olympus) Zeus’s blessings were active: Resistance and Strength II. Apollo, Artemis and Demeter accepted their offerings and Ares refused wheat (the right behaviour); Apollo’s Champion blessings (Regeneration II, Night Vision, Speed) were active at 41 favor.
  • The temples and the shrine are photographed from a client; seven of the nine temples and shrines generate on their own in a plains world; the Artemis and Poseidon temples need a forest and a beach.

Not yet tested:

  • Using an altar block with a held item (the click path).
  • Favor kept across death or a server restart (it is set to be kept; not yet checked in Olympia).
  • The blessings of Athena, Poseidon and Hermes at each stage were not individually inspected.
