A Minecraft mod
An add-on that changes the game. This one adds marble blocks, temples, four gods, a few creatures and a labyrinth.
A temple and an altar
A temple is a building for a god. Its altar is a block: hold an offering (say, a gold ingot for Zeus) and use it, and the god gives you a blessing.
Favor
A score each god keeps for you. More offerings, more favor, and the god names you higher: Unknown Mortal, Recognized, Favored, Blessed, Champion of Olympus.
The Thread of Ariadne
In the myth, a thread let Theseus find his way out of the labyrinth. Here it is an item that remembers where you tied it and points the way back.

Divine favor

Each god takes offerings at its altar. Every offering adds favor, favor moves you up five stages, and each stage from Favored on adds a blessing.

  1. 0Unknown Mortal
  2. 3Recognized
  3. 8Favored
  4. 18Blessed
  5. 36Champion of Olympus

The points are the thresholds in the mod’s own data file.

Status

Seen in a running Minecraft 1.21.4 game:

  • The mod loads on a Fabric 1.21.4 server with no data errors.
  • Six of the seven structures generate on their own in a flat plains world and the game’s /locate finds them (the Temple of Poseidon needs a beach and was placed by command).
  • Offering, favor stages, blessings, the Thunderbolt’s favor gate and the Thread of Ariadne work through the same code the items and altars use.
  • A Minotaur placed by its labyrinth hunted a player through the maze and reached them.

Not yet tested:

  • Using an altar by hand (the same code path was tested through a command).
  • Eating ambrosia, brewing the potions, and the Thunderbolt by a real right-click.
  • Fighting a cyclops or a hoplite, and either spawning on their own.
  • A fight with the Minotaur in real gear.
  • Anything in multiplayer, or a server restart.
