Creatures

Bronze, one eye, goat legs and a bull

Five creatures from the design’s Phase 3, each defined in a small data file (mobs/*.json) from which the mod’s classes, models, textures, eggs and loot are generated. Every one has its own model, built from 30 to 100 small moving parts with a texture packed and painted by the generator.

Hoplite

A citizen-soldier: a bronze Corinthian helmet with a swaying red horsehair crest, a muscle cuirass, greaves, a leather pteruges skirt, a round bronze shield on one arm and a spear in the other hand. It does not burn in sunlight and calls no reinforcements.

Hoplite: facts
Stats24 health (12 hearts), 4 attack damage, 6 armour
SpawnsPlains: groups of 1 to 3; set to patrol the Greek ruin and to guard the temples of Zeus and Athena.
DropsObol, Iron Nugget, Emerald
MeasuredIn a flat test world: reached a player 12 blocks away in 5.0 s; first hit took 3 health on Easy; effects on the player: none. (One run each; shadw-mod playtest mobtest.)
Spawn eggIn the Spawn Eggs tab, and /summon olympia:hoplite

Cyclops

A hunched one-eyed brute with a shaggy pelt over its shoulders, a heavy brow and tusks, a ragged hide loincloth and a spiked club of one great log. Hard to push, and its blows throw you into the air.

Cyclops: facts
Stats70 health (35 hearts), 8 attack damage, 4 armour
SpawnsWindswept hills, stony peaks, savanna plateau: 1 at a time, weight 3.
DropsBone, Cyclops Bloodstone, Iron Ingot, Obol
MeasuredIn a flat test world: reached a player 12 blocks away in 6.0 s; first hit took 5 health on Easy; effects on the player: none. (One run each; shadw-mod playtest mobtest.)
Spawn eggIn the Spawn Eggs tab, and /summon olympia:cyclops

Satyr

A goat-legged forest dweller with curling ram horns, a goatee and a set of pan-pipes at its lips. It has its own model: digitigrade goat legs with hooves, swaying ears and tail. Neutral: it leaves you alone until you strike it, then its blows make you queasy (Nausea).

Satyr: facts
Stats22 health (11 hearts), 3 attack damage, 0 armour, Nausea I for 6 s on hit
SpawnsForest, meadow, plains: 1 to 3 at a time, weight 6.
DropsLeather, Sweet Berries, Laurel
MeasuredIn a flat test world: did not attack until struck; first hit took 2.5 health on Easy; effects on the player: Nausea. (One run each; shadw-mod playtest mobtest.)
Spawn eggIn the Spawn Eggs tab, and /summon olympia:satyr

Gorgon

A serpent-bodied swamp hunter with a mane of twelve living snakes (each a chain of four moving parts), tusks, bronze gauntlets with claws and golden wings. It has its own model and a tail that slithers as it moves. Each blow slows you to a crawl, tires your arms and blinds you for a moment: a petrification of sorts, without the stone.

Gorgon: facts
Stats40 health (20 hearts), 5 attack damage, 2 armour, Slowness VI for 4 s, Mining Fatigue III for 4 s, Blindness I for 2 s on hit
SpawnsSwamp, mangrove swamp: 1 at a time, weight 4.
DropsBone, Emerald, Poisonous Potato
MeasuredIn a flat test world: reached a player 12 blocks away in 6.0 s; first hit took 3.5 health on Easy; effects on the player: Blindness, Mining Fatigue, Slowness. (One run each; shadw-mod playtest mobtest.)
Spawn eggIn the Spawn Eggs tab, and /summon olympia:gorgon

Minotaur

The bull-headed thing of the labyrinth: great curving horns, red eyes, a golden nose ring, a shaggy neck ruff, a gold-studded belt and a double-headed labrys. See its page. It has no natural spawn: the labyrinth places it.

Minotaur: facts
Stats140 health (70 hearts), 8 attack damage, 6 armour
SpawnsNone; the labyrinth places one.
DropsMinotaur Horn, Ambrosia, Diamond, Obol
MeasuredIn a flat test world: reached a player 12 blocks away in 3.0 s; first hit took 5 health on Easy; effects on the player: none. (One run each; shadw-mod playtest mobtest.)
Spawn eggIn the Spawn Eggs tab, and /summon olympia:minotaur

Status

Seen in a running Minecraft 1.21.4 game:

  • All five load, render with their own models and are photographed from front, three-quarter, side, back and close up.
  • A test harness measured each in a running game: time to reach a player, the first hit, the effects it gives and its drops (the numbers above).
  • The Minotaur’s behaviour was watched in play (see the labyrinth page).

Not yet tested:

  • Hoplites, cyclopes, satyrs and gorgons spawning on their own, and a real fight with any of them.
  • How the satyr and gorgon move in a fight (animation was checked only in still photographs).
  • Whether the numbers are fun: that needs a person.
