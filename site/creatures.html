Creatures

Bronze, one eye, and a bull

Three creatures from the design’s Phase 3. They use the zombie model with skins painted for the mod; the Minotaur’s horns are painted on the head, not modelled.

Hoplite

A citizen-soldier in a bronze Corinthian helmet and breastplate with a sword and shield. As written in the code (not yet seen in a fight): it does not burn in sunlight and calls no reinforcements.

Hoplite: facts
Stats24 health (12 hearts), 4 attack damage, 6 armour
SpawnsPlains: groups of 1 to 3, weight 8; set to patrol the Greek ruin and to guard the temples of Zeus and Athena.
DropsObol, Iron Nugget, Emerald
Spawn eggIn the Spawn Eggs tab, and /summon olympia:hoplite

Cyclops

A one-eyed brute, 1.4 times your size. As written: hard to push, and its blows throw you into the air.

Cyclops: facts
Stats70 health (35 hearts), 8 attack damage, 4 armour
SpawnsHills and peaks: one at a time, weight 3.
DropsBone, Cyclops Bloodstone, Iron Ingot, Obol
Spawn eggIn the Spawn Eggs tab, and /summon olympia:cyclops

Minotaur

The bull-headed thing of the labyrinth. See its page. It has no natural spawn: the labyrinth places it.

Minotaur: facts
Stats140 health (70 hearts), 8 attack damage, 6 armour
SpawnsNone; the labyrinth places one.
DropsMinotaur Horn, Ambrosia, Diamond, Obol
Spawn eggIn the Spawn Eggs tab, and /summon olympia:minotaur

Status

Seen in a running Minecraft 1.21.4 game:

  • All three load, render with their skins and are summoned and photographed.
  • The Minotaur’s behaviour was watched in play (see the labyrinth page). The stats are the ones in the data file, read at build time.

Not yet tested:

  • Hoplites and cyclopes spawning on their own, and a fight with either.
  • Their drops (the tables are written, not yet watched).
  • Custom models and animations: they reuse the zombie’s.
