Labyrinth

The way down

A stoa of marble columns stands over a ladder. Thirteen blocks down is a maze of three-wide corridors, built fresh from a seed, with a hall at its centre and a Minotaur in the hall. It does not wait there.

What it is

The labyrinth: facts
Size61 by 61 blocks, 21 high; 15 by 15 cells; corridors 3 wide and 3 high
Variants3, each a different maze
PlacementThe top ground layer is the pavilion’s floor; the maze is 13 blocks below. Set to generate in plains, meadows, savannas and forests, one in every 40 by 40 chunks.
Variant 02751 floor tiles; the walk from the ladder to the Minotaur’s hall is 43 blocks; 6 chests
Variant 12748 floor tiles; the walk from the ladder to the Minotaur’s hall is 43 blocks; 6 chests
Variant 22742 floor tiles; the walk from the ladder to the Minotaur’s hall is 163 blocks; 6 chests
TreasureChests at the farthest dead ends and one in the hall: Ambrosia, Obol, Diamond, Gold Ingot, Cyclops Bloodstone, Golden Apple, White Marble, Enchanted Golden Apple
The threadA chest in the pavilion holds a Thread of Ariadne. Use it where you tie it, use it anywhere to be told the way back.

The Minotaur

140 health, 8 damage, 6 armour, scale 1.5. It hunts: it picks the nearest player within 128 blocks, without needing to see them, and walks the maze toward them. In the open it charges (Speed III for 3 seconds); close in, it slams the ground for 6 damage and a shove; below half health it enrages: faster and harder. It never despawns and has a boss bar.

It took three fixes to make it find a player in the maze: its target goal still wanted line of sight; floor lanterns blocked the corridors; and one-wide corridors stopped a creature its size at every corner. It now reaches a player standing 163 blocks of corridor away in about a minute.

Status

Seen in a running Minecraft 1.21.4 game:

  • The labyrinth was placed in a world with room underground and the maze explored from a client; the connectivity of every variant was checked from the structure files.
  • The Minotaur, placed by the structure, charged (Speed III seen), slammed (it took a player from full health to zero in about six seconds with Regeneration running), enraged below half health (speed up, damage up), and hunted a player through the maze to reach them.
  • Its boss bar shows on screen (it is in the photographs, from a long way off: a boss bar shows to every player the game tracks the creature for).

Not yet tested:

  • A fight with real armour and weapons, and whether the damage is fair.
  • Natural generation of the whole maze in a normal world (in a flat world with no depth, only its entrance exists).
  • Multiple floors, traps, rotating walls and puzzles: not in this slice.
