Blocks

Marble for builders

Twelve stones, four stair-and-slab families, three column orders, and a few things to put in a city. Every stone has a stonecutter recipe. The recipes are written and checked by a linter; none has been cut in play.

The twelve stones

  • White Marble

    The base stone: pale, with thin grey veins.

    Stonecutter, from Calcite

  • Polished Marble

    Smooth, with a worn edge and a sheen line, for floors and plinths.

    Stonecutter, from White Marble

  • Weathered Marble

    Grey-green with moss patches, for old walls.

    Stonecutter, from White Marble

  • Cracked Marble

    One long crack through white stone, for ruins.

    Stonecutter, from White Marble

  • Black Marble

    Near-black with gold veins, for the Underworld and the Minotaur’s court.

    Stonecutter, from White Marble

  • Pink Marble

    Rose-white stone with pale veins.

    Stonecutter, from White Marble

  • Veined Marble

    White with strong blue-grey veining.

    Stonecutter, from White Marble

  • Golden Marble

    Warm gold stone with light veins.

    Stonecutter, from White Marble

  • Marble Bricks

    Offset marble bricks, like a real wall.

    Stonecutter, from White Marble

  • Marble Tiles

    A checker of white and grey tiles.

    Stonecutter, from White Marble

  • Greek Mosaic

    A Greek key (meander) band in blue and gold.

    Stonecutter, from White Marble

  • Greek Roof Tile

    Overlapping terracotta tiles for pitched roofs.

    Stonecutter, from Terracotta

Columns and the rest

  • Doric Column

    Plain flutes and a plain base: the oldest order.

    Crafting: X·/·X·/·X; X = White Marble

  • Ionic Column

    Finer flutes with a blue and gold band under the scrolls.

    Shapeless: Doric Column, Lapis Lazuli

  • Corinthian Column

    The finest flutes and a gold-and-olive leaf capital.

    Shapeless: Doric Column, Gold Nugget, Olive Leaves

  • Amphora

    A terracotta storage jar with a black-figure runner.

    Crafting: T·T·/·T·T·/·TTT; T = Terracotta

  • Greek Fire Brazier

    A bronze bowl of fire. Light level 14.

    Crafting: C·C·/·BCB·/·BBB; C = Copper Ingot, B = Marble Bricks

  • Laurel

    A small laurel plant with gold berries; grows on grass, dirt, sand and gravel.

  • Olive Log

    Grey twisted bark with a ringed end.

  • Olive Leaves

    Silver-green leaves with gaps; sometimes drop a sacred olive.

Status

Seen in a running Minecraft 1.21.4 game:

  • Every block loads, shows its own texture, and its item shows in the inventory and tab.
  • The blocks in these photographs were placed in the game and photographed from a client; the plants and leaves render with cutouts.

Not yet tested:

  • Mining speeds and tool requirements.
  • Placing stairs and slabs in every orientation and corner (the block states are generated for all of them).
  • Crafting any recipe by hand.
